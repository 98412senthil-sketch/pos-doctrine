# POS Constitution

The POS Constitution defines the sovereign laws that govern all AI-driven decision systems operating under POS.

## Article I — Belief Governance
All AI outputs are treated as belief distributions, not facts.

## Article II — Structural Governance
All governance is applied to latent regimes, not individuals.

## Article III — Collapse Physics
All enterprises are governed by Structural Instability Index (SII) and collapse-distance geometry.

## Article IV — Decision Sovereignty
No decision is automated without explicit sovereign human ownership.

## Article V — Lawful Automation
All actions are classified into lawful, forbidden, and escalatable actions.

## Article VI — Drift Governance
All regime migrations must be continuously monitored and governed.

## Article VII — Counterfactual Responsibility
All major decisions must be tested through counterfactual simulations.

## Article VIII — Auditability
All decisions must be recorded in immutable decision ledgers.

## Article IX — Executive Awareness
All executives must maintain real-time civilization health visibility.

## Article X — Institutional Alignment
POS aligns with regulatory, risk, and public-interest governance frameworks.
